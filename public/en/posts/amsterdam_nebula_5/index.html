The Amsterdam Nebula 5

Distant Tears
2 min | Translations:
  • العربية

Why does the sky begin to cry when summer begins negotiating with winter, in autumn?

Why does the sky become so depressed here?

The story begins with a line written by a Dutchman on the wall of a church on the island across the water.

In it, he complained about why he could not return to the nest of the one he loved.

" Alle vogels zijn aan hun nesten begonnen, behalve ik en jij. Waar wachten we nog op? "

He carved the words with his fingernails and felt no pain.

The pain inside him was stronger. Pain disappears inside pain, just as one shadow disappears into another.

He wrote the words and stood on the shore of the North Sea.

And there, he wept.

If you throw a single drop of liquid into the sea, the whole sea becomes that drop.

But the stranger poured many drops onto that shore.

The sea changed.

The sea became angry, and it has remained angry ever since.

From that day on, it kept sending its clouds to carry the stranger’s tears back to his land, to the nest he had wanted to return to.

The sea tears itself apart into clouds.

And the clouds weep for the sea they left behind.

They travel away, pouring the stranger’s tears over Amsterdam and the lands around it, and the tears return once again to that unhappy sea.

The sea wishes those tears would stay there.

For it had sworn to carry whatever remained of that stranger back to his nest.

Some part of that stranger still wants to return.

And until the North Sea itself reaches his nest, or dries up completely , the sea will keep longing to be there.

The sea, too, is a stranger.
