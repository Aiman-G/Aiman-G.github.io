There are many birds in Amsterdam that have lost their toes.

You see them standing on the ground, hunched over themselves.

They lose their feet to the rubbish humans leave behind.

A piece of thread gets caught around a foot. The wound becomes infected, and the thread slowly eats away at the foot until there is almost nothing left.

Birds can fly.

But that swollen, injured foot forces them back to the ground.

They keep limping, as though the pain itself prevents them from flying.

Does pain in the feet make them forget that they have wings?
